Our Story

A space rooted
in genuine connection.

Gaia Wellbeing was founded by Yasmin Magdy - Senior Clinical Psychologist, Marriage & Couples Therapist, and Addiction Counselor - with one vision: to build a therapy center in Egypt where clients feel truly safe, seen, and supported.

Drawing from CBT, DBT, EFT, and psychodynamic approaches, our team of 12+ specialists works across individual, couples, child, and family therapy - always placing the human relationship at the heart of care.

We are proud members of ICEEFT and the Gottman Referral Network - two of the most respected bodies in relational therapy worldwide.

Our Approach

Science-led.
Human-first.

Every Gaia therapist is trained in at least two evidence-based modalities and works from a deeply relational stance - because the therapeutic relationship itself is the most powerful agent of change.

We integrate cognitive, behavioral, and psychodynamic approaches with cultural sensitivity, ensuring care that genuinely resonates within the Egyptian and broader Middle Eastern context.
